02 / CARRY CONTEXT

Keep the useful part
of the previous session.

When work moves between sessions, the goal and the supporting evidence need to travel together. The public context-pack method starts with a specific task and selects material that helps the next worker act.

03 / INSPECT EVIDENCE

Ask what supports
the completion claim.

A finished message, a passing check, and a release are different events. Consulate’s public orchestration methods keep the requirement and its evidence visible through that distinction.

{% include motion.html id="evidence" %}

A record you can inspect

Begin with an observable result. Record the revision and input being checked, then keep the check’s outcome and the independent review alongside it.

Know what a test proves

A local test shows that what it covered worked on that machine. It doesn't show the change is merged, deployed, or right in a workflow nobody tested.
